cauterize - nidhogg (MARKERS SPREAD) leaves the arena, reappearing on the north or east edge and doing a charge through the arena

horrid roar - creates puddles under four random players, and gives markers to four other players
	dodge the puddles and dash, and spread out to avoid overlapping the markers

cauterize + horrid roar

touchdown (RAIDWIDE) - nidhogg slams down, doing raidwide damage

hot wing/tail - turns to a random player, casting this ability
	wing (TO/STAY CENTER) - two large aoes to the sides of him, stay in line with the boss
	tail (TO SIDES) - a line aoe through the arena in the direction he's facing, move to the sides

loops from cauterize until <80% hp

Add phase:
- Party starts on Brobinyak, then Falak, then Dragon
- One player (usually ranged DPS) needs to bait the Dragon's fireballs by staying the furthest away from it at all times
- Brobinyak tank should provoke the Falak after Brobinyak is dead, to avoid Falak tank getting too many vulns

mortal chorus + final chorus - a big transition raidwide

high jump (DROP PUDDLES) - marks four random players with aoes that leave behind puddles, take these to the edges

geirskogul (AVOID LINES) - a line aoe from each of the four puddles, as well as the boss

soul tether (OT TETHER PIACK UP AND MITIGATE)- a purple tether on a random player that needs to be intercepted by the OT, use a cooldown, it's a buster

ala morn (TANKBUSTER) - tankbuster

nidhogg's fang/claw (TANKSWAP) - creates a red claw and blue fang that chain to three random players each
	MT will ALWAYS get a blue chain, meaning a tankswap is needed
	the claw can only be damaged by red-chained players taking damage, the fang must be killed normally
	if blue-chained players take damage, they heal the fang, so avoid this at all costs



orbs (RED CHAIN PEOPLE PICK ORBS) - three orbs spawn randomly around the arena, exploding and dealing damage in an aoe when a player runs into them red-chained players need to take these to damage the claw

eye of the tyrant (RED CHAINS IN STACK) - a stack marker on a random blue-chained player, have red players stack with them NO OTHER BLUE-CHAINED PLAYERS CAN TAKE THIS STACK. PEOPLE WITHOUT CHAINS ALSO OUT

fang/clawbound (TANKSWAP NEW CHAINS) - the chains are randomly reassigned again, requiring another tank swap
loops until fang & claw die or enrage
