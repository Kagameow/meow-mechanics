## Tank

- Swap at **2-3 fire breath stacks**.
- Tank it on the EDGE - it has frontal cone cleave, offtank and tank ono different sides. 

## Core Mechanics
### Searing Wind (healers)
- Healer runs far away. Because they will EXPLODE WITH KNOCKBACK 3 TIMES
- Go to the safe marker
### Eruptions 
- dpses are rotating clockwise to dodge 3 eruption
- offtank can get only one eruption, avoid
### Chains
- Two players (dps and offtank) linked → stay **close together**.

### Radiant Plums (fire puddles)
- Only one safe spot, look for marker 

### Nail Phases
- **75%:** 4 nails  
- **50%:** 7 nails  
- **15%:** 12 nails + 1 big nail  (melee lb it?)
- Party can survive **2-3 overlapping explosions**.
