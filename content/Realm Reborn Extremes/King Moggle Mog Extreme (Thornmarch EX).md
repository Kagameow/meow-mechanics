**[Whiskerwall Kupdi Koop](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Whiskerwall_Kupdi_Koop "Whiskerwall Kupdi Koop")** (Paladin): Has 3 different basic single target attacks, one of the attacks will steal a small amount of HP. Mark this moogle with an *"X"*.
**[Woolywart Kupqu Kogi](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Woolywart_Kupqu_Kogi "Woolywart Kupqu Kogi")** (Ranger): Has no aggro table and will not move unless he is starting a combo attack. He uses a basic attack, "Moogle Eye Shot" which will fire 3, 4, or 5 attacks at a target marked with a multi-colored reticle. Mark him with a *"5"*.
**[Pukla Puki the Pomburner](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Pukla_Puki_the_Pomburner "Pukla Puki the Pomburner")** (Black Mage): Has no aggro table, don't try to control or tank her. She only uses 1 ability “Pom Flare” which is interrupted by attacking her 5 times. Failure to interrupt this means you will wipe. Mark her with a *"4"*.
**[Furryfoot Kupli Kipp](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Furryfoot_Kupli_Kipp "Furryfoot Kupli Kipp")** (White Mage): he is the healer that heals the other moogles. Mark with a *"3"*
**[Pukna Pako the Tailturner](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Pukna_Pako_the_Tailturner "Pukna Pako the Tailturner")** (Thief): Has no aggro table. Will randomly jolt around the map stabbing people. This can become a one-shot mechanic if you let her get too many buffs. Mark her with a *"1"*.
**[Ruffletuft Kupta Kapa](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Ruffletuft_Kupta_Kapa "Ruffletuft Kupta Kapa")** (Warrior): Has 2 basic single target attacks. Mark him with the *"O"*.
**[Puksi Piko the Shaggysong](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Puksi_Piko_the_Shaggysong "Puksi Piko the Shaggysong")** (Bard): Has an attack bonus song to other moogles and a damage song that does AoE around her. Somewhat responsive to hate. Mark her with **"2"**.

### King Buffs
- Keep King **away from other Moggles** to avoid buffing them.

- Have your tanks pick which of the PLD/WAR mog they are going to hold onto. When it get 3 stacks it is time to switch. The stacks are buffs given out by the king when he wiggles his butt. It is important to make sure the stacks fall off before switching. Tanks will Provoke The King when the opposing tanks add receives 3 stacks.

- If you get marked for moogle-go-round, run to the middle of the arena so the healers will be in range of you. Nothing is more annoying to healers than having to try and ensuna/leeches someone who isn't in range. It is probably best to have both healers sort their party list to be the same, have one start from the top and one from the bottom of the list to make sure they are not trying to remove the same debuff.

- If you are a tank please keep to the outside of the arena. You should never get close to the other mini-mogs and especially never get close to the other tank's add. Be vocal about your movement. Sometimes the king will get a buff off on a small mog, just do everything you can to never buff #5 (RNG) or #1 (THF). They get really strong, very fast. Do not cross over the middle and never cross paths.
