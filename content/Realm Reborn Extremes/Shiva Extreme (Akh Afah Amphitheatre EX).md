#### Unarmed Form

After using a weapon, Shiva will perform 2 unarmed attacks before using another weapon.
**Dreams of Ice**: Shiva gains a permanent, stackable [![](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/mediawiki/images/0/0c/Damage_up_icon1.png)](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Damage_Up "Damage Up") **[Damage Up](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Damage_Up "Damage Up")** buff. This serves as an enrage timer.
**Icicle Impact**: Circles appear on the ground in various patterns, and after a moment, the circles disappear and icicles drop down, dealing heavy damage to all players hit.
#### Sword Form
Shiva's auto attacks inflict stacks of **Blunt Resistance Down** on the main tank. The tank will take more damage from her auto attacks when she switches back to Staff Form.

- **Tanks**: When Shiva equips her sword, she will immediately use Icebrand. Face this attack towards the rest of the party to share/split the damage.

**Icebrand**: Conal attack that deals a total of 18k damage shared across the players that are hit.
**Glacier Bash**: Conal attack that deals heavy damage and stuns anyone caught in it. The tank can avoid it by running through Shiva after she starts casting it.
**Heavenly Strike**: Knockback from Shiva.
**Whiteout**: Untelegraphed donut AoE.
**Rotation: Ice Brand → Glacier Bash → Whiteout** (Heavenly Strikes are used randomly throughout)

#### Staff Form
Shiva's auto attacks inflict stacks of **Slashing Resistance Down** on the main tank. The tank will take more damage from her auto attacks when she switches back to Sword Form.

- When Shiva equips her staff, she will immediately cast **Hail Storm**. **DO NOT** wait to run out to the edges - it should be your **INITIAL** reaction.
**Hail Storm**: Ice AoEs will spawn on players' heads. Spread out to avoid hitting each other.
**Absolute Zero**: Raid-wide.

#### Bow Form

Only in Phase 3.
Shiva's auto attacks are unaffected by Slashing/Blunt Resistance Down while in this form but deal increased damage.

  **[![](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/mediawiki/images/thumb/a/a8/Glass_Dance.png/20px-Glass_Dance.png)](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Glass_Dance "Glass Dance") [Glass Dance](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Glass_Dance "Glass Dance")**: Used at the start of Bow Form. Fires icicles in a 270 degree cone around her, dealing 10k damage to all players hit. Your entire party needs to stand directly behind her to avoid the damage.

**Avalanche**: Places a mark above a random player. After a moment Shiva will fire a line AoE at the target, knocking back the marked player and anyone nearby. The knockback can be cancelled with   [![](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/mediawiki/images/thumb/0/06/Arm%27s_Length.png/20px-Arm%27s_Length.png)](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Arm%27s_Length "Arm's Length") [Arm's Length](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Arm%27s_Length "Arm's Length") or   [![](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/mediawiki/images/thumb/1/11/Surecast.png/20px-Surecast.png)](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Surecast "Surecast") [Surecast](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Surecast "Surecast"). It is used about 20 seconds after start of Bow Form.

- Move her close to the north edge of the arena. Have the marked player stand behind Shiva while the rest of the party stands in front of her. This will cause the player to be knocked back harmlessly towards the south of the room but not far enough to hit the wall and become frozen.
  
  
For the first 20% of the fight you will be fighting an _easier mode of what's to come_. Shortly after the pull she will change to a random weapon, either **Staff** or **Sword**. After that she will always choose the other.  
Compared to the last phase, the aoes at the start are usually hard to dodge. Since there won't be much damage generally, it is recommended you take one aoe hit without risking to take multiple ones. The aoes in the later phases are always dodgeable.  
Hailstorm, her **Staff-initial-move** which I will go into detail later, will also target all players instead of only 4-5 in the first phase.  
The first phase lasts up to 80% of her HP and will always have 2 weapon changes.

Shiva's Stances - Staff Mode

One of the weapons she will use is the **Staff**. Right after she takes out her **Staff**, she will cast **Hailstorm**, an attack that puts _multiple aoes over the players' heads_, including the tank.  
To avoid massive damage it is recommended you avoid stacking. They work similarly to Ramuh's **Thunderstrike**. Spread out and _make sure your circle doesn't cross another player_ (it doesn't matter if it only crosses the other player's cirlce).  
After that she will do about **4 map-wide aoes** that hit all players for medium damage. This will need immediate aoe-healing attention in later phases.  
  
While she has the staff up, she will be inflicting _blunt damage_ on the tank + a **slashing resistance debuff** with her auto-attacks. She doesn't use any cleave-attacks so you don't have to worry about getting your party hit if you have to turn her.  
_The slashing resistance debuffs stack up to 8 times and last 2 minutes whenever she re-applies it._ This will affect her damage during Sword mode.  
  
It looks like this: [![](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/52117160237127537/AB0FD76D21B819F414DD07F39A848B96C5EBEAB2/)](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/52117160237127537/AB0FD76D21B819F414DD07F39A848B96C5EBEAB2/)  
  
Once she's finished with her last aoe, she will hit a few auto-attacks on the tank before putting away the staff.

Shiva's Stances - Sword Mode

The other weapon she will be using is the **Sword**. After equipping her **Sword** she will always use **Icebrand** first. **Icebrand** will hit for 18k, however _the damage is spread among all the players hit_ with it. To efficiently survive it, have the tank turn her into the party and get everyone hit by it. Then move right back to position.  
  
After **Icebrand** she will use **Glacial Bash**. This attack is similar to Garuda's **Slipstream**. _It can (and has to be) avoided by walking behind her after she starts casting it_. This is the only cleave-attack that can kill your party if you turn her before she casts it. It will do _up to 7k damage and stun everyone who's hit_. Make sure you face her away after Icebrand!  
  
After a short skill-break she will use **Whiteout**, _a donut-aoe that can be avoided by standing close to her_. It will not kill you but it does a lot of damage which should be avoided. Make sure you stand close.  
  
Immediately after **Whiteout** she will use a knockback skill that kicks everyone away. _After adds the tank has to make sure not to get knocked into the wall by this_. Either avoid it by using **Tempered Will** or move behind her after **Whiteout**.  
  
During **Sword mode** she will inflict _slashing damage + a blunt debuff on the tank with her auto attacks_. The debuff will affect the damage the tank takes from the staff and the adds! When playing with 2 tanks, the tank with the slashing debuff is supposed to tank the adds.  
  
The blunt debuff looks like this: [![](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/52117160237130597/4B90451265B5D47E846C35C41E80AA459E519C97/)](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/52117160237130597/4B90451265B5D47E846C35C41E80AA459E519C97/)  
  
Once she's done with her knockback skill, she will hit the tank a few times before putting away her weapon.

Add-Phase and Diamond Dust

Shortly after hitting her below 80% of her HP, she will move to the center of the arena and _summon 4 adds_. These adds need to be killed in order to survive her signature-move **Diamond Dust**.  
  
_Everyone should stack up in the center_ and give the offtank time to pick up the adds, then aoe them down. If you're not sure about your damage you can use a **level 1 caster Limit Break** to inflict extra damage, but _it should be easy to kill them without_.  
  
The 4 circles of the adds have no meaning other than an indicator of the adds that are still alive. They don't do damage to you and they don't debuff the adds. Even if people say something else, **trust me - I tried.**  
  
During add phase, Shiva will always be in **Sword mode** - make sure the MT hits everyone with **Icebrand** to avoid his death. Also make sure to face her outside again after that to avoid Glacial Bash on the party.  
  
After Shiva disappears she will coat everything with her **Diamond Dust** move. _This is the last chance you got to kill the adds_. If they aren't dead by the time everyone gets frozen, you will die from Diamond Dust.  
  
Having succeeded in killing the adds, the last phase will start.

What Lies Beyond the First Diamond Dust

Starting from here, her last phase will start. You're now running against time to kill her before she kills you. _The arena is now surrounded by a snowstorm. If you move too close to the edge of the arena you will end up in an ice rock._  
  
She still has all the mechanics from before and will now use 2 circle-aoes while she changes her weapon to sword or staff.  
_These aoes always have 4 safespots near the edge of the map_. They move every time as well. As an example, if the safespots were arranged like an "x" on the first aoe, they will be arranged like a "+" right after that. Since you will have to deal with these aoes while she does her intial-weapon-move you need to be careful.  
If she changes to staff, which she always does first after Diamond Dust, you will have to deal with the circle aoes, _the 4 safespots and the Hailstorm aoe on 4-5 players_. If everyone stacks on the same safespot, they're all dead.  
  
_To counter this, create pairs at the start of the match and assign a spot for every pair. Every pair has to make clear who is going to stand on the innermost spot of the safespot and who will standing in the outermost spot of the safespot, in case both of them have an aoe above them. Take into consideration that the circle aoe and the safespots move every second time. Make sure everyone moves clockwise or counterclockwise with their safespots to avoid stacking in panic._  
  
This is an example how the safespots look like and how they move. Please not that the first aoe of the two in succession (the one before she changes her weapon) doesn't have an aoe in the center!  

[![](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/52117160239765256/A85FCA523A4288353C5D43F896A47D2C2D09670D/)](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/52117160239765256/A85FCA523A4288353C5D43F896A47D2C2D09670D/)[![](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/52117160239766743/3522228C3900020C36170C6BD9E3E2C1D4571D5A/)](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/52117160239766743/3522228C3900020C36170C6BD9E3E2C1D4571D5A/)-  
  
She will also gain a new skill which she will use randomly, called **Permafrost**. It looks like a maelstrom on the whole map. If you see it, stop moving right away. Skills are fine but if you move during Permafrost you will slip and slide across the map. If you reach the edge of the arena, you will get frozen and end up in a rock. Don't move until the floor isn't frozen anymore. This lasts for like 3-4 seconds.  
  
After her first weapon she will do a circluar aoe similar to **Titan's circle-bomb-pattern**. Either have everyone stack in the middle and take the hit or move to the last circle and wait for the first circle to disappear.  
After the circle-pattern she will introduce a new weapon, **the bow**, which isn't too difficult but needs serious attention.  
  
Please note that _every circular aoe inflicts a **Vulnerability Up** debuff_ which lasts for a minute and stacks multiple times. The only time when your tank should be taking one is before she picks up the bow, if possible. _A tank with 4 stacks **Vulnerability Up** will drop like butter._ It's not worth healing.  

Shiva's Stances - Bow Mode

**The Bow mode**, which _always comes after the **Titan-like circle pattern**_, does not inflict any debuffs on the tank and isn't affected by his debuffs. However she has an _increased critical hit rate while carrying the bow, making skills like Awareness a good option._  
  
_Right when she changes to bow she will do a 270° aoe with a safespot behind her_. The aoe has no indicator and is executed right after she finishes changing. Everyone not standing in the safespot will die from massive damage. _The tank can't turn her as soon as she starts changing her weapon_. To avoid people's deaths from not paying attention, _have the tank take one of the circular aoes instead of dodging them to make sure she's facing outside._

Have the player stand right behind Shiva and let everyone move away from him! She will cast a line-aoe on this player and knock everyone who's hit back in the process. _If Shiva is tanked near the edge and the marked person stays close, you can avoid getting frozen by the arena walls. If the tank gets the mark he will have to be freed from the ice rock, avoid it with Tempered Will or have him buffed with Stoneskin and Adloquium so he will take 0 damage._
