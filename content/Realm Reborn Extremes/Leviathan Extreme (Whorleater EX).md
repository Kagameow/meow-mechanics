- One tank on **Tail** (reflects magical).

## Core Mechanics
### Blue Orbs
- Offtank pick up and mitigate explosion.

### Levi Dashes
- West/East corner splashes → prepare **arena slide** (run opposite).
- Elsewhere splash → **dash incoming** → move away.

### Elemental Converter
- Use directly after dash.

### Wave Tooth AoE
- If allowed to cast, circle causes **fear → likely death**.
