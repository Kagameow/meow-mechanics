## Tank
- Gungnir = heavy tank buster → mitigate well.

## Core Mechanics
- Spread out: Odin targets 3 players with AoE + slow.
- Kill **spear adds** immediately (constant raid pulses).

## Final Phase
- At **15% HP**, Odin begins long Hard Enrage cast.
- Save all burst and cooldowns for final 15%.
