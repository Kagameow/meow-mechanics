title: "The Navel Extreme (Titan)"
---
## Tank
- **Mountain Buster** gives vuln. Swap at **3 stacks**.
- Tank Titan at edge facing outward.

## Core Mechanics
### Jails
- 2 players jailed. Keep them apart to avoid tether.
- Free both quickly.

### Upheaval
- Titan crouches → large knockback. Position for safe landing.

### Granite Gaolers ADDs
- OT picks them up.
- Kill them in a corner and close together to reduce AoE puddles

### General
- Higher damage versions of normal Titan mechanics → dodge everything.
