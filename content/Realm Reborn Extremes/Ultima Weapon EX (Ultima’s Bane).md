## Tank
- Swap at **3–4 Viscous Aetheroplasm stacks**. Invulns do NOT work.
- Position carefully during knockback mechanics.
- Help soak orbs if needed.

## Core Mechanics
### Aetheric Boom (50%, 30%, 15%)
- Spawns **2 / 3 / 4 tethered orb pairs**.
- Break tether by soaking **1 orb per pair** → the other despawns.
- **2 DPS/healers** share or **1 tank** solos.
- Watch for knockbacks. They are super stupid and random

### Other Notes
- Kill **Magitek Bits** quickly.
- If needed, sacrifice players to prevent orb collision (better than wipe).
