## Tank
- Tanks grab **3 orbs** to gain Shock Resistance.
- Swap when tank's resistance buff is expiring. Pick up on 15 second

## Core Mechanics
### Orbs
- Grabbing 3 orbs also removes **Rolling Thunder** tethers.
- Keep roughly **3 orbs** in arena at all times.

### Fear Targets
- Marked players freeze then walk toward Ramuh.
- Bring them to **south marker** → hit by blue circle → cleanses fear.

### Blue Circles
- Drop orbs.
- Followed by donut AoE in water + outer ring.

### Rolling Thunder
- Two players tethered.
- Each action = damage. Remove by grabbing **3 orbs**.
- If someone dies, tether jumps to new player.

### Add Phase
- Burn adds but follow orb/tether mechanics throughout.
