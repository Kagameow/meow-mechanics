title: "The Howling Eye Extreme (Garuda)"
---
## Tank
- Untelegraphed frontal cleave + Slipstream (sidestep).
- MT pulls Garuda + Suparna north; OT pulls Chirada south.

## Core Mechanics
### Rocks
- Hide behind rocks for Mistral Song.
- Avoid damaging rocks with AoE.

### Plumes
- **Satin Plume** → sleep → kill first.
- **Razor Plume** → AoE → kill.
- **Spiny Plume**: safest strat = kill it. Thermal Low at 3 stacks wipes party.

### Add Phase
- Kill Chirada → then Suparna.
- Aerial Blast damage scales with remaining rock count.

### After Aerial Blast
- If Garuda leaves → **Tank LB** on landing.
