Magitek ray (MEGA BEAM) - fires a dodgeable beam towards a random player
arm and hammer (TANK BUSTER)

Atomic ray (DPS TO CORNERS) - raidwide and marks all dps for orb drops (PROXIMITY)
	dps should spread away from the boss as the orbs explode afterwards
	assign a corner for each dps to spread to, melees north and ranged south
	
Missile (PRAY, OUT) - marks one player with a prey and gives them a nasty dot

LOAD (LOOK AT THE BIG SCREEN) - begins loading the program on the big screen and casts ability
	there are two different timelines that it can do
	each timeline is a series of connected mini-phases

### Bibliotaph

load (bibliotaph) + demon simulation (SPRINT TO CIRCLES) - spawns four summoning circles that require four players in each. Have the melees take the north and east circles while the ranged take south and west need to lock them before demon simulation is cast


### Dadaluma 

Shockwave - KNOCKBACK, go close to boss or resist

Missile Simulation + Chakra Burst (CLOCK SPOTS, AVOID ROCKETS) - dodge the missiles while standing in the yellow towers. Assign clock spots for players and take the tower on your spot. Dps use their corner spots for the towers, and supports take the cardinal towers
	
Run program (dadaluma) (PICK UP ADD) - spawns dadaluma, should be tanked close to the boss
magitek ray + wave cannon (BEAM; MARKER TO SIDE, SINK) - dodge the ray and whoever gets the blue marker goes to the side of dadaluma away from the boss

TANKBUSTER + wave cannon (TANKBUSTER; MARKER TO SIDE, SINK)
missile (PRAY OUT)
magitek ray (BEAM)

### Ultros 

load (ultros) (SPREAD) - does big spread aoes on each player, go to rough clock spots

diffractive plasma (RAIDWIDE)

Tentacle Simulation (STACK BEHIND, THEN OUT) - spawns tentacle aoes under every player that will do line aoes in the direction they were facing after a delay stack up behind the boss and face towards it, then move to the side

Run program (ultros) (PROVOKE ADD, EVERYONE TO CORNER)  - spawns ultros, drag the boss over to ultros corner,

Stoneskin - INTERUPT

Air Raid (OUT?) - the boss flies up, becoming untargetable and fires at a random player this can be baited into the corner

main cannon (PROXIMITY,  TO OTHER CORNER) - after a few shots, there will be a proximity aoe on the bait spot so make sure you've run to the opposite corner in time

diffractive plasma (RAIDWIDE)


### Air Force 

Load (air force) - (OUT FAST),  point-blank aoe that stuns

Missile simulation + run program (air force) (DPS ADDS IN CORNERS, AVOID ROCKETS) - dodge missiles while dps get tethered by air force adds

bomb deployment (DEFUSE, LOOK FOR SHINY) - two bombs spawn one loop lights up, and you stand in that shiny loop to defuse

Skip Program (virus) - tethers each tank to a healer, and each dps to another dps, and gives them magnet debuffs plus another debuff
	these debuffs can be baited by staying far away/close respectively
	searing wind - a massive aoe blast that is given to players with OPPOSITE charges
		if you get this, go to the edges of the arena right into the corners
		support tether can go west, dps tether can go east
	abandonment - need to be stacking with at least one other player at the end of the debuff
		if you get this stay close to the boss which should be in mid
		dps slightly behind the boss, supports slightly in front
		make sure to stay tightly stacked with the player of the same role, they are who you stay with for abandonment
	aether rot - afflicts one player who has abandonment
		will pass to another player if they move into you
		cannot take it again after it has been taken from you
		keep passing this around
		five? passes are needed
